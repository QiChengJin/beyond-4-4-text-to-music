## Notes

- **4/4 cultural prompts** use electronic dance and reggae genres (house, techno, jersey club, disco, reggae) because they are unambiguously 4/4. Unlike waltz (3/4) or jig (6/8), there is no single cultural name that uniquely means 4/4 — this asymmetry is itself a finding worth discussing.
- **5/4 cultural prompts** are weaker than other time signatures because shared cultural vocabulary for 5/4 is limited in Western music. This reflects the cultural bias being studied.
- Jersey club and Bulgarian folk are more niche references; the model may have less training data for these, which is a potential confound to acknowledge.
